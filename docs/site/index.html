TideLine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

TideLine is developed by erikhardlyworking. Privacy questions can be submitted through https://github.com/erikhardlyworking/tideline/issues. Please do not put private location details in a public issue.

What stays on your device

TideLine calculates astronomical tide predictions on your device from bundled ocean-tide data. It has no Internet permission, developer server, advertising, analytics, account system, or crash-reporting service. The app does not transmit your location or settings to the developer or third parties.

If you choose current location, TideLine requests approximate location from Android. The latest coordinates, accuracy and fix time are stored locally to select nearby tide data. A fixed location and its optional name are also stored locally. Appearance and forecast settings are retained separately for each widget. Older fixes are replaced; TideLine does not record a travel history. Temporary calculation caches stay in app memory.

Optional location while the app is closed

Background following is off by default. If you enable it, TideLine accesses approximate location in the background, including when the app is closed, to keep opted-in tide widgets near your location as you move. A disclosure appears before permission setup. Android background location permission is required on Android 10 and later. Only the latest fix is retained on this device. Turn this option off to stop TideLine's background location requests. Fixed-location and foreground-only modes remain available.

TideLine requests occasional network/passive system locations, not continuous GPS tracking. Android controls availability, precision and delivery. The operating system's location service may use network services under your device's location settings; TideLine itself does not upload location or make forecast requests.

Retention, security and deletion

Settings and the latest fix are kept in Android's app-private storage. App backup is disabled. Data remains until replaced, its widget is removed, or you clear app storage or uninstall TideLine. Removing a widget deletes that widget's settings; app-wide new-widget defaults may retain the latest selected location. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all TideLine settings and saved locations. You can revoke location permissions through Android settings at any time. A revoked permission prevents future access; it does not automatically erase an already saved fix.

There are no TideLine accounts or remote records to delete. If you contact the developer through GitHub, GitHub processes that communication under its own privacy policy. Opening an external website uses your browser, separately from TideLine's offline calculations.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The current policy is included in the app and in the project repository.
